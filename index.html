About

A little technical background

I'm a software engineer with 10+ years of experience architecting and shipping large-scale web platforms, end user products, and internal tooling. I specialize in taking products from idea to production, and I care as much about getting them into users' hands quickly as I do about getting the architecture right.
Building and shipping is what I do best.

During my tenure at Coinbase, I was a tech lead on Growth, where I drove greenfield builds of the platforms every marketing and product team uses to reach customers. Today they power over a billion messages a week. I designed the upsell framework that turned weeks of engineering into a same-day launch, and led the effort to scale the marketing server to 10x traffic through the 2021 bull run and a Super Bowl ad.

Before that, at ConnectWise, I was the architecture lead on migrating the flagship product to React, owned the company's design system, and built the Customer Portal from the ground up. Outside of work, I co-founded Automelodia, an AI music generation app we took from zero to a publicly launched, paid product.

I work across the stack, with deep experience in React and TypeScript on the frontend, Python on the backend, and AWS cloud infrastructure. I leverage AI-assisted development with Claude Code to move fast without cutting corners.
